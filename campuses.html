Our Campuses

Sta. Ana

Our Sta. Ana Campus is located in the heritage district of Manila. The school is known for its strong academic programs, community involvement, and love for culture and heritage.

Greenhills

Our Greenhills Campus in San Juan City offers its students a creative and fun environment to spark their imagination, love of learning, and service to the community.

Fairview

Our Fairview Campus in Quezon City delivers quality Montessori education to its thriving community of young families.

Las Pinas

Our Las Pinas Campus pays homage to the area's cultural history, the birthplace of the Sarao jeepney, and the unique 19th-century Bamboo Organ. Its welcoming atmosphere promotes the development and learning of students.

O.B. Montessori Center

Our campuses offer accessible, high-quality Montessori education in a nurturing environment for learning, creativity, and development. Come and visit our facilities to experience what each OBMC campus can offer your child and family.
